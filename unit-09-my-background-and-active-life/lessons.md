# Unit 09 — My Background and Active Life: Practice Lessons (Day 1–6)

This personalized unit turns your real background, work, and interests into language you can reuse in introductions and conversations.

## Daily method (25–35 minutes)

1. **Retrieve (3–5 min):** answer aloud without notes.
2. **Read (5 min):** read the characters-only dialogue twice, then summarize it.
3. **Understand (5 min):** answer in complete Mandarin sentences.
4. **Build (7–10 min):** complete the controlled and translation work before checking.
5. **Speak (8–12 min):** make original statements and complete the roleplay.
6. **Check (2–3 min):** open each panel only after attempting its section.

- **New targets:** #1–#10

- **Required output:** give a four-sentence personal timeline and ask two follow-up questions.
- **Finish when:** you can connect Maryland, UMD, and Chicago without notes.

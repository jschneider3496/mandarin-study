## Review strategy upgrades

### Weekly Review Mode (mix units)

- After Unit 2: mix Units 1–2
- After Unit 3: mix Units 1–3
- After Unit 4: mix Units 1–4
- After Unit 6: mix Units 1–6, with emphasis on service interactions
- After Unit 8: mix Units 5–8, with emphasis on problems, advice, and decisions

Suggested weekly session (25–35 min):
- 8 min vocab lightning round (timed)
- 10 min reading recap (summarize 2 readings in 2–3 sentences each)
- 10–15 min mixed roleplay (one scenario touching 2 contexts)

### Fast recall drills (timed)

- Answer within ~3 seconds.
- If you hesitate: repeat the corrected sentence 3 times.

### Cross-unit mixing scenarios

- Introduce yourself → then order food
- Work trip travel recap → then restaurant meeting
- Talk about work → then weekend travel plan
- Feel sick at work → ask for leave → explain next steps
- Run errands → compare products → request a receipt or return
- Tech problem → explain the issue → discuss a study plan

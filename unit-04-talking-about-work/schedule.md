# Unit 04 — Schedule (6 days)

Rules:
- Quick scan all 40 key words daily.
- Use the focus set for deeper speaking.
- Day 3+ always review earlier days.

## Day 1 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #1–#10 (work basics)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - `在…工作`  
  - responsibility: `负责…`
- **Reading:** Day 1 “你做什么工作？”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Introduce your job in 2–3 sentences.  
  2) Use ≥3 words: 公司 / 工作 / 负责 / 项目  
  3) Ask GPT: “What are you responsible for?”

## Day 2 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #11–#22 (time + ability/need + plans)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - meeting time: `什么时候开会？`  
  - `应该/可以/能/会` modal choices
- **Reading:** Day 2 “什么时候开会？”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Ask and answer when the meeting is (include 今天/明天/现在).  
  2) Use ≥3 words: 什么时候 / 开会 / 重要 / 老板  

## Day 3 🟡 (Review Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #23–#30 (finish/start/continue + busy/overtime/leave)
- **Review:** summarize Day 1 in 2 sentences.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - completion: `完成 + 了`  
  - “not yet” chunk: 还没有 (from reading glossary)
- **Reading:** Day 3 “任务进度”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Tell GPT your progress: what you finished / what you haven’t finished.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 完成 / 需要 / 时间 / 应该 / 可以  

## Day 4 🟡 (Review Day 2 + Day 1)
- **Focus:** pick 10 weak words across #1–#40.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - asking for help: `你可以帮忙吗？`  
  - request + soften: `解释一下…`
- **Reading:** Day 4 “帮个忙”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Ask for help with a “problem” and specify what you need.  
  2) Use ≥3 words: 问题 / 帮忙 / 需要 / 解释  

## Day 5 🔴 (Review Days 1–3)
- **Review:** rapid-fire 8 prompts (EN → ZH): meeting time, busy, finish task, ask for leave, etc.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - leave request: `想请假…可以吗？`  
  - condition: `如果…`
- **Reading:** Day 5 “请假一天”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Roleplay: ask the boss for leave + promise a plan to finish tasks.  
  2) Use ≥5 earlier words (Day 1–3).  

## Day 6 🔴 (Review Days 1–5)
- **Review:** 6-sentence work recap (today’s tasks + meeting + problem + plan).
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - email workflow: `发送/回复`  
  - time: `…以后…就…` (from reading)
- **Reading:** Day 6 “邮件和回复”
- **Capstone roleplay (3–5 minutes):**
  - You and GPT: meeting planning → task status → help request → email follow-up.  
  - Constraints: 2–3 sentences/turn; reuse ≥8 ⭐ Core words; include at least 2 “如果…” sentences.

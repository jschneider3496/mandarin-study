# Unit 03 — Schedule (6 days)

Rules:
- Quick scan all 40 key words daily.
- Use the focus set for deeper speaking.
- Day 3+ always review earlier days.

## Day 1 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #1–#10 (travel plan + transport + ticket)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - `打算 + 去 + place`  
  - sequencing: `先…再…`
- **Reading:** Day 1 “做计划”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) “I plan to travel this weekend.”  
  2) Say: first go to A, then go to B.  
  3) Use ≥3 words: 打算 / 计划 / 先 / 再  

## Day 2 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #11–#20 (book hotel, room, luggage, sightseeing)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - completion: `V + 了` (买了/订了)  
  - `到…` arrival
- **Reading:** Day 2 “买票”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Translate: “I bought the ticket.”  
  2) Answer in 2–3 sentences: how you go + how long it takes.  
  3) Use ≥3 words: 买 / 票 / 到 / 行李  

## Day 3 🟡 (Review Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #21–#30 (opinions + 了/过 + because/so)
- **Review:** summarize Day 1 in 2 sentences.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - `到了以后…` (after arriving…)  
  - `先…再…` with actions
- **Reading:** Day 3 “订酒店”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Say your arrival plan: check in first, then visit.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 到 / 以后 / 先 / 再 / 入住  

## Day 4 🟡 (Review Day 2 + Day 1)
- **Focus:** pick 10 weak words across #1–#40.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - describing experiences with `了`  
  - contrast: `但是…`
- **Reading:** Day 4 “风景很好”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Say 2–3 sentences about what you did today on a trip.  
  2) Include 1 feeling: 开心/累 + `觉得`.  
  3) Use ≥3 words: 参观 / 拍照 / 风景 / 觉得  

## Day 5 🔴 (Review Days 1–3)
- **Review:** rapid-fire 8 prompts using 了/过 + travel nouns.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - experience: `去过…`  
  - cause/effect: `因为…所以…`
- **Reading:** Day 5 “去过吗？”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Ask GPT: “Have you been to X?” and respond with `去过/没去过`.  
  2) Explain why you liked/didn’t like it using 因为…所以…  

## Day 6 🔴 (Review Days 1–5)
- **Review:** tell a 6-sentence trip recap using as many key words as possible.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - recap with `了` (actions) + `觉得` (opinion)  
  - “next time”: `下次…还想…再…`
- **Reading:** Day 6 “旅行小结”
- **Capstone roleplay (3–5 minutes):**
  - You and GPT talk about a recent trip: planning → transport → hotel → sightseeing → recap.  
  - Constraints: 2–3 sentences/turn; reuse ≥8 ⭐ Core words; include at least 2 sentences with 因为…所以…

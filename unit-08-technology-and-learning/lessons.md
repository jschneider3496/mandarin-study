# Unit 08 — Technology and Learning: Practice Lessons (Day 1–6)

This unit preserves all 40 targets in `vocab.md` and reorganizes the original readings and schedule around retrieval and production.

## Daily method (25–35 minutes)

1. **Retrieve (3–5 min):** answer the warm-up aloud without notes.
2. **Read (5 min):** read the characters-only dialogue twice and summarize it once.
3. **Understand (5 min):** answer the dialogue questions in full Mandarin sentences.
4. **Build (7–10 min):** translate the English prompts before checking the model.
5. **Speak (8–12 min):** complete the production constraints and roleplay.
6. **Check (2–3 min):** open the panel beneath the section, correct errors, and repeat corrected Mandarin twice.

Each numbered section has a collapsed support panel. Do not open it until you have attempted the exercise.

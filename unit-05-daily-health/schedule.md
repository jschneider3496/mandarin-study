# Unit 05 — Schedule (6 days)

Rules:
- Quick scan all 40 key words daily.
- Use the focus set for deeper speaking.
- Day 3+ always review earlier days.

## Day 1 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #1–#10 (health and symptoms)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - condition/state: `身体不太舒服`  
  - possible reason: `可能是因为…`
- **Reading:** Day 1 “最近身体怎么样？”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Describe how you feel today in 3–4 sentences.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 身体 / 舒服 / 头疼 / 压力  
  3) Ask GPT what it does when it feels tired.

## Day 2 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #11–#20 (doctor, medicine, rest, movement)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - sequence: `先…再…`  
  - advice: `最好…`
- **Reading:** Day 2 “去药店买药”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Roleplay: tell a friend your symptoms and decide whether to go to the pharmacy or hospital.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 感冒 / 发烧 / 药店 / 休息

## Day 3 🟡 (Review Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #21–#28 (diet, habits, pressure)
- **Review:** summarize Day 1 in 2 sentences.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - past habit: `以前经常…`  
  - future change: `以后要…`
- **Reading:** Day 3 “改生活习惯”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Explain one habit you want to change and why.  
  2) Use ≥5 words: 习惯 / 生活 / 注意 / 保持 / 健康

## Day 4 🟡 (Review Day 2 + Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #29–#35 (stop, must, should, frequency)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - interruption: `先停一下`  
  - necessity: `必须…`
- **Reading:** Day 4 “缓解压力”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Ask for a short break and explain how it will help.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 停 / 必须 / 缓解 / 安静

## Day 5 🔴 (Review Days 1–3)
- **Focus:** pick 10 weak words across #1–#40.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - condition: `如果…就…`  
  - doctor's advice: `医生说…`
- **Reading:** Day 5 “看医生以后”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Roleplay: after seeing a doctor, explain the result and next steps to a coworker.  
  2) Use ≥6 earlier words and one `如果…就…` sentence.

## Day 6 🔴 (Review Days 1–5)
- **Review:** 6-sentence health recap (symptoms + cause + advice + new plan).
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - plan order: `首先…其次…`  
  - benefit/result: `这样…会…`
- **Reading:** Day 6 “一周健康计划”
- **Capstone roleplay (3–5 minutes):**
  - You and GPT: tired at work → symptoms → pharmacy/doctor → habit change → weekly health plan.  
  - Constraints: 3–4 sentences/turn; reuse ≥8 ⭐ Core words; include one concrete next step.

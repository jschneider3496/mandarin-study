# Unit 06 — Schedule (6 days)

Rules:
- Quick scan all 40 key words daily.
- Use the focus set for deeper speaking.
- Day 3+ always review earlier days.

## Day 1 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #1–#10 (errands, bank, post office, cost)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - errand plan: `有几件事情要办`  
  - ordering: `首先…然后…最后…`
- **Reading:** Day 1 “今天要办几件事”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Describe three errands in order.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 办 / 银行 / 邮局 / 费用

## Day 2 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #11–#20 (payment, queue, exchange/return)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - ability/options: `可以…也可以…`  
  - waiting time: `大概要…分钟`
- **Reading:** Day 2 “在银行排队”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Roleplay: ask a staff member what to do first and how to pay.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 号码 / 排队 / 刷卡 / 现金

## Day 3 🟡 (Review Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #21–#30 (shopping basics, quality, size, color)
- **Review:** summarize Day 1 in 2 sentences.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - problem report: `我发现…写错了`  
  - condition: `只要…就…`
- **Reading:** Day 3 “邮局地址写错了”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Explain a small errand mistake and ask how to fix it.  
  2) Use ≥5 words: 地址 / 邮件 / 换 / 麻烦 / 可以

## Day 4 🟡 (Review Day 2 + Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #31–#40 (choice, recommendation, necessity)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - comparison: `A比较…，但是B…`  
  - suggestion: `我建议你…`
- **Reading:** Day 4 “比较价格和质量”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Compare two products and choose one.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 价格 / 质量 / 合适 / 选择

## Day 5 🔴 (Review Days 1–3)
- **Focus:** pick 10 weak words across #1–#40.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - return request: `可以退货吗？`  
  - alternative: `先…如果…再…`
- **Reading:** Day 5 “想退货”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Roleplay: return or exchange a product politely.  
  2) Use ≥6 earlier words.

## Day 6 🔴 (Review Days 1–5)
- **Review:** 6-sentence errand recap (places + payment + problem + result).
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - completed action: `买了 / 拿到了 / 等了`  
  - expense explanation: `因为这是公司的费用`
- **Reading:** Day 6 “公司费用和发票”
- **Capstone roleplay (3–5 minutes):**
  - You and GPT: bank errand → post office problem → shopping comparison → return/exchange → company invoice.  
  - Constraints: 3–4 sentences/turn; reuse ≥8 ⭐ Core words; include one polite request.

# Unit 07 — Schedule (6 days)

Rules:
- Quick scan all 40 key words daily.
- Use the focus set for deeper speaking.
- Day 3+ always review earlier days.

## Day 1 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #1–#10 (opinions, suggestions, agreement)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - opinion: `我觉得… / 我的看法是…`  
  - suggestion: `我建议…`
- **Reading:** Day 1 “你的看法是什么？”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Give an opinion about a plan and suggest one change.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 看法 / 建议 / 讨论 / 问题

## Day 2 🟢
- **Vocab focus:** #11–#20 (clear explanation, reasons, decisions)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - correction: `我不是…只是…`  
  - clarity: `说清楚 / 说明原因`
- **Reading:** Day 2 “我不是反对你”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Politely disagree or clarify without sounding harsh.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 同意 / 反对 / 原因 / 确实

## Day 3 🟡 (Review Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #21–#30 (pros, cons, correctness)
- **Review:** summarize Day 1 in 2 sentences.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - misunderstanding: `产生了误解`  
  - explanation: `我的意思是…`
- **Reading:** Day 3 “产生了误解”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Explain a misunderstanding and restate your real meaning.  
  2) Use ≥5 words: 误解 / 重点 / 清楚 / 正确 / 错

## Day 4 🟡 (Review Day 2 + Day 1)
- **Vocab focus:** #31–#40 (nuance connectors)
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - pros/cons: `优点是…缺点是…`  
  - concession: `虽然…但是…`
- **Reading:** Day 4 “优点和缺点”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Compare one option's advantages and disadvantages.  
  2) Use ≥4 words: 优点 / 缺点 / 简单 / 复杂

## Day 5 🔴 (Review Days 1–3)
- **Focus:** pick 10 weak words across #1–#40.
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - willingness: `不是不愿意，只是…`  
  - decision sequence: `先…再决定…`
- **Reading:** Day 5 “希望你愿意试试”
- **Speaking (constraints):**
  1) Roleplay: persuade someone to try a method, then respond to their concern.  
  2) Use ≥6 earlier words.

## Day 6 🔴 (Review Days 1–5)
- **Review:** 6-sentence discussion recap (view + reason + concern + decision).
- **Grammar targets:**  
  - final decision: `我的意见是…`  
  - follow-up condition: `如果效果不好，再…`
- **Reading:** Day 6 “做最后决定”
- **Capstone roleplay (3–5 minutes):**
  - You and GPT: share opinions → clarify misunderstanding → compare pros/cons → make a decision.  
  - Constraints: 3–4 sentences/turn; reuse ≥8 ⭐ Core words; include one gentle disagreement.

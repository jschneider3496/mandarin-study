# Unit 01 — Answer Key and Reading Support

Use this file only after attempting the corresponding section in `reading.md`. Open-ended responses may have many correct answers.

## Day 1

### Dialogue support

1. *Nǐhǎo! Wǒmen hái bú rènshi ba? Wǒ xiān zuò ge zìwǒ jièshào. Wǒ jiào Lín Zhōu, xìng Lín.* — Hello! We have not met yet, right? Let me introduce myself first. I’m Lin Zhou; my surname is Lin.
2. *Nǐhǎo, wǒ jiào Ānnà. Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.* — Hello, I’m Anna. Nice to meet you.
3. *Nǐ de Zhōngwén míngzi jiào shénme?* — What is your Chinese name?
4. *Wǒ de Zhōngwén míngzi jiào Ānnà. Nǐ kěyǐ jièshào yíxià zìjǐ ma?* — My Chinese name is Anna. Can you introduce yourself?
5. *Dāngrán kěyǐ. Wǒ láizì Hángzhōu, shì cóng Hángzhōu lái Shànghǎi de.* — Of course. I’m from Hangzhou; I came to Shanghai from Hangzhou.
6. *Wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng, xiànzài zhù zài Shànghǎi. Nǐ ne?* — I used to live in Beijing and now live in Shanghai. How about you?
7. *Wǒ yě zhù zài Shànghǎi, jiù zhù zài gōngsī fùjìn.* — I also live in Shanghai, right near the company.
8. *Yuánlái wǒmen zhù de bù yuǎn. Rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng!* — It turns out we do not live far apart. Nice to meet you!

### Suggested comprehension answers

1. 林舟姓林，他的名字叫林舟。
2. 林舟来自杭州。
3. 安娜以前住在北京，现在住在上海。
4. 因为他们都住在上海，而且住得不远。
5. Answers vary; for example: 你现在做什么工作？

### Controlled practice

1. 自我介绍
2. 姓
3. 来自
4. 介绍
5. 你叫什么名字？
6. 我从杭州来。

### English → Mandarin

1. 你好，我叫安娜。
2. 你姓什么？
3. 我来做个自我介绍。
4. 我来自芝加哥，但是现在住在上海。
5. 这是我的朋友，我来给你们介绍一下。

## Day 2

### Dialogue support

1. *Lín Zhōu, nǐ xiànzài zuò shénme gōngzuò?* — Lin Zhou, what work do you do now?
2. *Wǒ mùqián zài yì jiā kējì gōngsī gōngzuò. Nǐ hái zài xuéxí ma?* — I currently work at a technology company. Are you still studying?
3. *Duì, wǒ xiànzài xuéxí Zhōngwén, wǒ de zhuānyè shì guójì shāngwù.* — Yes. I study Chinese now, and my major is international business.
4. *Nǐ de zhuānyè tīng qǐlái hěn yǒu yìsi. Nǐ wèishénme xuéxí Zhōngwén?* — Your major sounds interesting. Why do you study Chinese?
5. *Yīnwèi wǒ duì yǔyán bǐjiào yǒu xìngqù, yě xǐhuan rènshi bùtóng dìfang de rén.* — Because I am quite interested in languages and also like meeting people from different places.
6. *Xuéxí yǔyán yě shì wǒ de àihào, búguò wǒ gèng xǐhuan shuō, bú tài xǐhuan xiě.* — Studying languages is also my hobby, but I prefer speaking and do not like writing as much.
7. *Wǒ gēn nǐ bù yíyàng. Wǒ bǐjiào xǐhuan yuèdú, dànshì yě xiǎng duō liànxí kǒuyǔ.* — I’m different from you. I rather like reading, but I also want to practice speaking more.
8. *Nà wǒmen yǐhòu kěyǐ yìqǐ xuéxí, hùxiāng bāngzhù.* — Then we can study together and help each other later.

### Suggested comprehension answers

1. 林舟目前在一家科技公司工作。
2. 安娜现在学习中文，她的专业是国际商务。
3. 因为她对语言比较有兴趣，也喜欢认识不同地方的人。
4. 林舟更喜欢说，安娜比较喜欢阅读。
5. Answers vary. Include `更` and a reason.

### Controlled practice

1. 目前
2. 兴趣
3. 爱好
4. 比较
5. 更
6. 我现在在工作。我的工作比较忙，但是我喜欢这份工作。
7. 我学习中文，但是我更想练习口语。

### English → Mandarin

1. 我目前在一家公司工作。
2. 你的专业是什么？
3. 我对语言有兴趣，学习中文也是我的爱好。
4. 我的工作比较忙，但是我喜欢这份工作。
5. 比起写，我更喜欢说中文。

## Day 3

### Dialogue support

1. *Nǐ píngshí gōngzuò hé xuéxí dōu hěn máng ma?* — Are you usually busy with both work and study?
2. *Gōngzuòrì bǐjiào máng, yīnwèi báitiān yào shàngkè, suǒyǐ wǒ yìbān wǎnshang fùxí.* — Weekdays are rather busy. Because I attend class during the day, I generally review at night.
3. *Nǐ jīngcháng zài jiā xuéxí, háishi qù túshūguǎn?* — Do you often study at home or go to the library?
4. *Wǒ píngshí zài jiā xué, dànshì zhōumò jīngcháng qù túshūguǎn. Nǐ zhōumò yìbān zuò shénme?* — I usually study at home, but often go to the library on weekends. What do you generally do on weekends?
5. *Wǒ xǐhuan yùndòng, dànshì zuìjìn gōngzuò máng, yùndòng bǐjiào shǎo.* — I like exercise, but work has been busy recently, so I exercise relatively little.
6. *Nà nǐ zhè ge zhōumò yǒu shénme jìhuà?* — What plans do you have this weekend?
7. *Wǒ juéde tiānqì hǎo dehuà, kěyǐ xiān qù pǎobù, zài zhǎo péngyou chīfàn.* — I think if the weather is good, I can run first and then meet a friend for a meal.
8. *Zhè ge jìhuà búcuò, yīnwèi yùndòng hé jiàn péngyou dōu hěn zhòngyào.* — That plan is good because exercise and seeing friends are both important.

### Suggested comprehension answers

1. 因为安娜白天要上课，所以她一般晚上复习。
2. 她平时在家学习，周末经常去图书馆。
3. 因为林舟最近工作忙，所以运动比较少。
4. 他计划先去跑步，再找朋友吃饭。
5. Answers vary. Give a comparison and a reason.

### Controlled practice

1. 但是
2. 因为；所以
3. 还是
4. 我工作很忙，但是我每天学习中文。
5. 因为周末我有时间，所以我经常做饭。
6. Answers vary; for example: 我们周六见，还是周日见？

### English → Mandarin

1. 我一般晚上学习。
2. 你周末经常工作吗？
3. 因为我今天很忙，所以我打算明天联系朋友。
4. 我喜欢出去，但是这个周末还是想在家休息。
5. 你觉得我们应该喝咖啡，还是一起吃饭？

## Day 4

### Dialogue support

1. *Ānnà, zhè shì wǒ de tóngshì Xiǎo Gù. Xiǎo Gù, zhè shì wǒ de péngyou Ānnà.* — Anna, this is my colleague Xiao Gu. Xiao Gu, this is my friend Anna.
2. *Nǐhǎo, hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. Lín Zhōu yǐjīng jièshào guo nǐ le.* — Hello, nice to meet you. Lin Zhou has already told me about you.
3. *Nǐhǎo! Tīngshuō nǐ yě duì yǔyán yǒu xìngqù, hái jīngcháng liànxí Yīngyǔ.* — Hello! I heard that you are also interested in languages and often practice English.
4. *Duì, wǒ dǎsuàn yǐhòu xuéxí Xībānyáyǔ. Nǐ hái zài xué Zhōngwén ma?* — Yes. I plan to study Spanish later. Are you still studying Chinese?
5. *Hái zài xué. Rúguǒ nǐ fāngbiàn dehuà, wǒmen zhōumò kěyǐ yìqǐ liànxí.* — I still am. If you are free, we can practice together this weekend.
6. *Hǎo a. Wǒ Zhōuliù yǐjīng yǒu jìhuà le, dànshì Zhōurì xiàwǔ fāngbiàn.* — Sure. I already have plans Saturday, but Sunday afternoon works.
7. *Nà nǐmen xiān jiā liánxì fāngshì, yǐhòu jiù kěyǐ zhíjiē liánxì le.* — Then exchange contact information first, and later you can contact each other directly.
8. *Hǎo. Rènshi xīn péngyou, hái néng yìqǐ xuéxí, zhēn búcuò.* — Great. Meeting a new friend and also being able to study together is really nice.

### Suggested comprehension answers

1. 小顾是林舟的同事。
2. 小顾打算以后学习西班牙语。
3. 安娜建议她们周末一起练习语言。
4. 小顾周六已经有计划，周日下午方便。
5. Answers vary; mention exchanging contact information or sending a message.

### Controlled practice

1. 已经；还
2. 打算
3. 如果；一起
4. 朋友；同事
5. 我周六不方便，但是周日方便，我们周日见吧。
6. 我们已经有对方的联系方式了，以后可以联系。

### English → Mandarin

1. 我已经认识她了，但是还不认识她的同事。
2. 我打算这个周末联系一个朋友。
3. 如果你方便的话，我们可以一起学习。
4. 我周六已经有计划了。周日方便吗？
5. 我们先加联系方式，以后保持联系吧。

## Day 5

### Dialogue support

1. *Xiǎo Gù, wǒ xiǎng bǎ wǒ de péngyou Měilíng jièshào gěi nǐ. Tā láizì Xīnjiāpō, xiànzài zhù zài Shànghǎi.* — Xiao Gu, I want to introduce my friend Meiling to you. She is from Singapore and now lives in Shanghai.
2. *Tā mùqián zài gōngzuò, háishi zài xuéxí?* — Is she currently working or studying?
3. *Tā yǐjīng kāishǐ gōngzuò le, zài yì jiā yínháng gōngzuò, búguò zhōumò hái xuéxí Zhōngwén.* — She has already started working at a bank, but still studies Chinese on weekends.
4. *Tā wèishénme xiǎng xué Zhōngwén? Shì yīnwèi gōngzuò ma?* — Why does she want to learn Chinese? Is it because of work?
5. *Gōngzuò shì yí ge yuányīn, dànshì tā zìjǐ yě duì yǔyán yǒu xìngqù.* — Work is one reason, but she is also personally interested in languages.
6. *Zhēn qiǎo! Wǒ píngshí yě xǐhuan xué yǔyán. Tā hái yǒu shénme àihào?* — What a coincidence! I usually like studying languages too. What other hobbies does she have?
7. *Tā jīngcháng pǎobù, yě xǐhuan zuòfàn, búguò bǐqǐ yùndòng, tā gèng xǐhuan gēn péngyou yìqǐ chīfàn.* — She often runs and likes cooking, but compared with exercise, she prefers eating with friends.
8. *Rúguǒ tā zhè ge zhōumò fāngbiàn, wǒmen kěyǐ liánxì tā, yìqǐ hē kāfēi.* — If she is free this weekend, we can contact her and have coffee together.

### Suggested comprehension answers

1. 美玲来自新加坡，现在住在上海。
2. 她目前在一家银行工作，周末还学习中文。
3. 因为工作，也是因为她对语言有兴趣。
4. 比起运动，她更喜欢跟朋友一起吃饭。
5. 小顾建议联系美玲，这个周末一起喝咖啡。

### Accuracy practice

1. 我来自美国。/ 我从美国来。
2. 我目前在上海工作。
3. 因为我喜欢中文，所以我每天学习。
4. 我已经认识他的朋友了，但是还不认识他的同事。
5. 如果你方便，我们就一起吃饭。

### English → Mandarin

1. 这是我的同事，我来介绍一下她。
2. 她来自新加坡，目前住在上海。
3. 她在一家银行工作，但是周末还学习中文。
4. 因为她对语言有兴趣，所以她经常跟朋友一起练习。
5. 如果她这个周末方便，我们打算请她喝咖啡。

## Day 6

### Dialogue support

1. *Nǐhǎo, zhèr yǒu rén zuò ma? Wǒ jiào Chén Míng, dì-yī cì lái cānjiā zhè ge huódòng.* — Hello, is this seat taken? I’m Chen Ming, and this is my first time attending this event.
2. *Nǐhǎo, wǒ jiào Ānnà. Zhèlǐ méi rén zuò. Nǐ láizì nǎr?* — Hello, I’m Anna. No one is sitting here. Where are you from?
3. *Wǒ láizì Chéngdū, búguò xiànzài zhù zài Sūzhōu, mùqián zài Shànghǎi gōngzuò. Nǐ ne?* — I’m from Chengdu, but now live in Suzhou and currently work in Shanghai. How about you?
4. *Wǒ cóng Jiānádà lái, xiànzài zài Shànghǎi xuéxí Zhōngwén. Nǐ píngshí yě xué yǔyán ma?* — I come from Canada and now study Chinese in Shanghai. Do you usually study languages too?
5. *Xué yìdiǎnr. Yīnwèi gōngzuò bǐjiào máng, suǒyǐ wǒ yìbān zhōumò xuéxí. Nǐ yǒu shénme àihào?* — A little. Because work is rather busy, I generally study on weekends. What hobbies do you have?
6. *Wǒ xǐhuan kàn diànyǐng hé pǎobù, dànshì zuìjìn gèng xǐhuan gēn péngyou yìqǐ zuòfàn.* — I like watching movies and running, but recently I prefer cooking with friends.
7. *Wǒ yě xǐhuan zuòfàn! Rúguǒ nǐ Zhōurì fāngbiàn, wǒmen kěyǐ yìqǐ qù cānjiā yí ge huódòng.* — I like cooking too! If Sunday works for you, we can attend an event together.
8. *Zhōurì wǒ yǐjīng yǒu jìhuà le, búguò Zhōuliù hái fāngbiàn. Wǒmen xiān jiā liánxì fāngshì ba.* — I already have plans Sunday, but Saturday still works. Let’s exchange contact information first.

### Suggested comprehension answers

1. 陈明是第一次来这里，他来参加一个活动。
2. 他来自成都，住在苏州，目前在上海工作。
3. 安娜现在在上海学习中文。
4. 两个人都喜欢做饭。
5. 因为安娜周日已经有计划了，所以他们可能周六见面。

### English → Mandarin

1. 很高兴认识你。你是第一次来这儿吗？
2. 我来自成都，但是目前住在苏州，在上海工作。
3. 因为工作比较忙，所以我一般周末学习。
4. 我喜欢看电影，但是最近更喜欢跟朋友一起做饭。
5. 我周日已经有计划了。如果周六方便，就联系我吧。

## How to use corrections

For every incorrect answer:
1. Read the corrected sentence aloud twice.
2. Close this file.
3. Say a new sentence with the same target or pattern.
4. Test it again later in the session with different information.

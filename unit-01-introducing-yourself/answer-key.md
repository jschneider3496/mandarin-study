# Unit 01 — Answer Key and Reading Support

Use this file only after attempting the corresponding section in `lessons.md`. Every model is shown as Hanzi → tone-mark pinyin → English. Open-ended responses may have many correct answers; those sections give one natural model.

## Day 1

### Dialogue support

1. 你好！我们还不认识吧？我先做个自我介绍。我叫林舟，姓林。<br>
   *Nǐhǎo! Wǒmen hái bú rènshi ba? Wǒ xiān zuò ge zìwǒ jièshào. Wǒ jiào Lín Zhōu, xìng Lín.*<br>
   Hello! We have not met yet, right? Let me introduce myself first. I am Lin Zhou; my surname is Lin.
2. 你好，我叫安娜。很高兴认识你。<br>
   *Nǐhǎo, wǒ jiào Ānnà. Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.*<br>
   Hello, I am Anna. Nice to meet you.
3. 你的中文名字叫什么？<br>
   *Nǐ de Zhōngwén míngzi jiào shénme?*<br>
   What is your Chinese name?
4. 我的中文名字叫安娜。你可以介绍一下自己吗？<br>
   *Wǒ de Zhōngwén míngzi jiào Ānnà. Nǐ kěyǐ jièshào yíxià zìjǐ ma?*<br>
   My Chinese name is Anna. Can you introduce yourself?
5. 当然可以。我来自杭州，是从杭州来上海的。<br>
   *Dāngrán kěyǐ. Wǒ láizì Hángzhōu, shì cóng Hángzhōu lái Shànghǎi de.*<br>
   Of course. I am from Hangzhou; I came to Shanghai from Hangzhou.
6. 我以前住在北京，现在住在上海。你呢？<br>
   *Wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng, xiànzài zhù zài Shànghǎi. Nǐ ne?*<br>
   I used to live in Beijing and now live in Shanghai. How about you?
7. 我也住在上海，就住在公司附近。<br>
   *Wǒ yě zhù zài Shànghǎi, jiù zhù zài gōngsī fùjìn.*<br>
   I also live in Shanghai, right near the company.
8. 原来我们住得不远。认识你很高兴！<br>
   *Yuánlái wǒmen zhù de bù yuǎn. Rènshi nǐ hěn gāoxìng!*<br>
   It turns out we do not live far apart. Nice to meet you!

### Suggested comprehension answers

1. 林舟姓林，他的名字叫林舟。<br>
   *Lín Zhōu xìng Lín, tā de míngzi jiào Lín Zhōu.*<br>
   Lin Zhou's surname is Lin, and his name is Lin Zhou.
2. 林舟来自杭州。<br>
   *Lín Zhōu láizì Hángzhōu.*<br>
   Lin Zhou is from Hangzhou.
3. 安娜以前住在北京，现在住在上海。<br>
   *Ānnà yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng, xiànzài zhù zài Shànghǎi.*<br>
   Anna used to live in Beijing and now lives in Shanghai.
4. 因为他们都住在上海，而且住得不远。<br>
   *Yīnwèi tāmen dōu zhù zài Shànghǎi, érqiě zhù de bù yuǎn.*<br>
   Because they both live in Shanghai and do not live far apart.
5. 你现在做什么工作？<br>
   *Nǐ xiànzài zuò shénme gōngzuò?*<br>
   What work do you do now?

## Day 2

### Dialogue support

1. 林舟，你现在做什么工作？<br>
   *Lín Zhōu, nǐ xiànzài zuò shénme gōngzuò?*<br>
   Lin Zhou, what work do you do now?
2. 我目前在一家科技公司工作。你还在学习吗？<br>
   *Wǒ mùqián zài yì jiā kējì gōngsī gōngzuò. Nǐ hái zài xuéxí ma?*<br>
   I currently work at a technology company. Are you still studying?
3. 对，我现在学习中文，我的专业是国际商务。<br>
   *Duì, wǒ xiànzài xuéxí Zhōngwén, wǒ de zhuānyè shì guójì shāngwù.*<br>
   Yes. I study Chinese now, and my major is international business.
4. 你的专业听起来很有意思。你为什么学习中文？<br>
   *Nǐ de zhuānyè tīng qǐlái hěn yǒu yìsi. Nǐ wèishénme xuéxí Zhōngwén?*<br>
   Your major sounds interesting. Why do you study Chinese?
5. 因为我对语言比较有兴趣，也喜欢认识不同地方的人。<br>
   *Yīnwèi wǒ duì yǔyán bǐjiào yǒu xìngqù, yě xǐhuan rènshi bùtóng dìfang de rén.*<br>
   Because I am quite interested in languages and also like meeting people from different places.
6. 学习语言也是我的爱好，不过我更喜欢说，不太喜欢写。<br>
   *Xuéxí yǔyán yě shì wǒ de àihào, búguò wǒ gèng xǐhuan shuō, bú tài xǐhuan xiě.*<br>
   Studying languages is also my hobby, but I prefer speaking and do not like writing as much.
7. 我跟你不一样。我比较喜欢阅读，但是也想多练习口语。<br>
   *Wǒ gēn nǐ bù yíyàng. Wǒ bǐjiào xǐhuan yuèdú, dànshì yě xiǎng duō liànxí kǒuyǔ.*<br>
   I am different from you. I rather like reading, but I also want to practice speaking more.
8. 那我们以后可以一起学习，互相帮助。<br>
   *Nà wǒmen yǐhòu kěyǐ yìqǐ xuéxí, hùxiāng bāngzhù.*<br>
   Then we can study together and help each other later.

## Day 3

### Dialogue support

1. 你平时工作和学习都很忙吗？<br>
   *Nǐ píngshí gōngzuò hé xuéxí dōu hěn máng ma?*<br>
   Are you usually busy with both work and study?
2. 工作日比较忙，因为白天要上课，所以我一般晚上复习。<br>
   *Gōngzuòrì bǐjiào máng, yīnwèi báitiān yào shàngkè, suǒyǐ wǒ yìbān wǎnshang fùxí.*<br>
   Weekdays are rather busy. Because I attend class during the day, I generally review at night.
3. 你经常在家学习，还是去图书馆？<br>
   *Nǐ jīngcháng zài jiā xuéxí, háishi qù túshūguǎn?*<br>
   Do you often study at home or go to the library?
4. 我平时在家学，但是周末经常去图书馆。你周末一般做什么？<br>
   *Wǒ píngshí zài jiā xué, dànshì zhōumò jīngcháng qù túshūguǎn. Nǐ zhōumò yìbān zuò shénme?*<br>
   I usually study at home, but often go to the library on weekends. What do you generally do on weekends?
5. 我喜欢运动，但是最近工作忙，运动比较少。<br>
   *Wǒ xǐhuan yùndòng, dànshì zuìjìn gōngzuò máng, yùndòng bǐjiào shǎo.*<br>
   I like exercise, but work has been busy recently, so I exercise relatively little.
6. 那你这个周末有什么计划？<br>
   *Nà nǐ zhè ge zhōumò yǒu shénme jìhuà?*<br>
   What plans do you have this weekend?
7. 我觉得天气好的话，可以先去跑步，再找朋友吃饭。<br>
   *Wǒ juéde tiānqì hǎo dehuà, kěyǐ xiān qù pǎobù, zài zhǎo péngyou chīfàn.*<br>
   I think if the weather is good, I can run first and then meet a friend for a meal.
8. 这个计划不错，因为运动和见朋友都很重要。<br>
   *Zhè ge jìhuà búcuò, yīnwèi yùndòng hé jiàn péngyou dōu hěn zhòngyào.*<br>
   That plan is good because exercise and seeing friends are both important.

### Controlled practice

2. 因为；所以<br>

3. 还是<br>
   *háishi*<br>

4. 我工作很忙，但是我每天学习中文。<br>
   *Wǒ gōngzuò hěn máng, dànshì wǒ měitiān xuéxí Zhōngwén.*<br>

5. 因为周末我有时间，所以我经常做饭。<br>

6. 我们周六见，还是周日见？<br>
   *Wǒmen Zhōuliù jiàn, háishi Zhōurì jiàn?*<br>

## Day 4

### Dialogue support

1. 安娜，这是我的同事小顾。小顾，这是我的朋友安娜。<br>
   *Ānnà, zhè shì wǒ de tóngshì Xiǎo Gù. Xiǎo Gù, zhè shì wǒ de péngyou Ānnà.*<br>
   Anna, this is my colleague Xiao Gu. Xiao Gu, this is my friend Anna.
2. 你好，很高兴认识你。林舟已经介绍过你了。<br>
   *Nǐhǎo, hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ. Lín Zhōu yǐjīng jièshào guo nǐ le.*<br>
   Hello, nice to meet you. Lin Zhou has already told me about you.
3. 你好！听说你也对语言有兴趣，还经常练习英语。<br>
   *Nǐhǎo! Tīngshuō nǐ yě duì yǔyán yǒu xìngqù, hái jīngcháng liànxí Yīngyǔ.*<br>
   Hello! I heard that you are also interested in languages and often practice English.
4. 对，我打算以后学习西班牙语。你还在学中文吗？<br>
   *Duì, wǒ dǎsuàn yǐhòu xuéxí Xībānyáyǔ. Nǐ hái zài xué Zhōngwén ma?*<br>
   Yes. I plan to study Spanish later. Are you still studying Chinese?
5. 还在学。如果你方便的话，我们周末可以一起练习。<br>
   *Hái zài xué. Rúguǒ nǐ fāngbiàn dehuà, wǒmen zhōumò kěyǐ yìqǐ liànxí.*<br>
   I still am. If you are free, we can practice together this weekend.
6. 好啊。我周六已经有计划了，但是周日下午方便。<br>
   *Hǎo a. Wǒ Zhōuliù yǐjīng yǒu jìhuà le, dànshì Zhōurì xiàwǔ fāngbiàn.*<br>
   Sure. I already have plans Saturday, but Sunday afternoon works.
7. 那你们先加联系方式，以后就可以直接联系了。<br>
   *Nà nǐmen xiān jiā liánxì fāngshì, yǐhòu jiù kěyǐ zhíjiē liánxì le.*<br>
   Then exchange contact information first, and later you can contact each other directly.
8. 好。认识新朋友，还能一起学习，真不错。<br>
   *Hǎo. Rènshi xīn péngyou, hái néng yìqǐ xuéxí, zhēn búcuò.*<br>
   Great. Meeting a new friend and also being able to study together is really nice.

4. 朋友；同事<br>

# Unit 01 — Practice Lessons (Day 1–6)

This unit uses the same 40 targets in `vocab.md`, but every day is organized around retrieval and production.

## Daily method (25–35 minutes)

1. **Retrieve (3–5 min):** say the warm-up answers aloud without notes.
2. **Read (5 min):** read the characters-only dialogue twice and summarize it once.
3. **Understand (5 min):** answer all dialogue questions in full Mandarin sentences.
4. **Build (7–10 min):** complete the model-sentence, controlled, and English→Mandarin exercises.
5. **Speak (8–12 min):** make original sentences and complete the roleplay.
6. **Check (2–3 min):** use `answer-key.md`, correct errors, and repeat corrected sentences twice.

Do not scan all 40 words before every lesson. Try retrieval first; open `vocab.md` only after identifying what you cannot recall.

Do not check pinyin, translations, or suggested answers until you have attempted the exercises.

## Day 1 🟢 — 第一次见面

- **New targets:** 你好 / 认识 / 自我介绍 / 叫 / 姓 / 名字 / 介绍 / 来自 / 从 / 住在
- **Patterns:** `我叫…，姓…` · `我来自… / 我从…来` · `你住在哪儿？`
- **Required output:** four-sentence self-introduction; three follow-up questions
- **Repetition goal:** distinguish `来自` from `从…来`
- **Finish when:** you can state your name, origin, and current location without notes

### 1. Quick production

Without reading the dialogue, say:
1. Hello and give your name.
2. Say where you come from.
3. Ask another person’s name and where they live.

### 2. Read first: characters only

- 林舟：你好！我们还不认识吧？我先做个自我介绍。我叫林舟，姓林。
- 安娜：你好，我叫安娜。很高兴认识你。
- 林舟：你的中文名字叫什么？
- 安娜：我的中文名字叫安娜。你可以介绍一下自己吗？
- 林舟：当然可以。我来自杭州，是从杭州来上海的。
- 安娜：我以前住在北京，现在住在上海。你呢？
- 林舟：我也住在上海，就住在公司附近。
- 安娜：原来我们住得不远。认识你很高兴！

**Mini glossary:** 先 first · 中文名字 Chinese name · 公司附近 near the company · 不远 not far

### 3. Dialogue questions

Answer in complete Mandarin sentences.
1. 林舟姓什么？他的名字是什么？
2. 林舟来自哪儿？
3. 安娜以前住在哪儿？现在呢？
4. 为什么安娜说他们住得不远？
5. 如果你是安娜，你还想问林舟什么？写一个新问题。

### 4. See the words again

Read aloud, then change one piece of information in each sentence orally.
1. 你好，我叫王宁，姓王。
2. 我的名字是李月，很高兴认识你。
3. 我来做个自我介绍：我来自成都，现在住在南京。
4. 我从美国来，但是现在住在中国。
5. 这是我的同事，我来介绍一下。

### 5. Controlled practice

Fill in each blank with a target word.
1. 我来做个________：我叫陈明。
2. A：你________什么？ B：我姓周。
3. 我________广州，现在住在北京。
4. 这是我的朋友，我来给你们________一下。

Rewrite as directed.
5. 我叫林舟。→ Ask for the other person’s name.
6. 我来自杭州。→ Say the same idea with `从…来`.

### 6. English → Mandarin

Translate before checking the key.
1. Hello, my name is Anna.
2. What is your surname?
3. Let me introduce myself.
4. I am from Chicago, but I live in Shanghai now.
5. This is my friend. I’ll introduce you two.

### 7. Create and speak

1. Give a four-sentence introduction using at least six new targets.
2. Create one sentence with `来自` and a different sentence with `从…来`.
3. Ask three questions that would help you get to know someone.
4. Roleplay: meet a new neighbor. Exchange names, hometowns, and current locations. Each person must ask a follow-up question.

**Exit ticket:** Without looking, say your name, origin, and current city in one connected answer.

## Day 2 🟢 — 现在做什么？

- **New targets:** 现在 / 目前 / 工作 / 学习 / 专业 / 兴趣 / 爱好 / 喜欢 / 比较 / 更
- **Recycle:** 叫 / 来自 / 住在
- **Patterns:** `目前在…工作/学习` · `我的专业是…` · `比起A，我更喜欢B`
- **Required output:** describe work or study; compare two interests
- **Repetition goal:** use `兴趣`, `爱好`, `比较`, and `更` in separate sentences
- **Finish when:** you can ask and answer four questions about work, study, and interests

### 1. Retrieval warm-up

Without looking back:
1. Introduce yourself in three sentences.
2. Ask where someone comes from and where they live now.
3. Say the same origin once with `来自` and once with `从…来`.

### 2. Read first: characters only

- 安娜：林舟，你现在做什么工作？
- 林舟：我目前在一家科技公司工作。你还在学习吗？
- 安娜：对，我现在学习中文，我的专业是国际商务。
- 林舟：你的专业听起来很有意思。你为什么学习中文？
- 安娜：因为我对语言比较有兴趣，也喜欢认识不同地方的人。
- 林舟：学习语言也是我的爱好，不过我更喜欢说，不太喜欢写。
- 安娜：我跟你不一样。我比较喜欢阅读，但是也想多练习口语。
- 林舟：那我们以后可以一起学习，互相帮助。

**Mini glossary:** 科技公司 technology company · 国际商务 international business · 口语 spoken language · 互相 each other

### 3. Dialogue questions

Answer in complete Mandarin sentences. Use a target word in every answer.
1. 林舟目前在哪儿工作？
2. 安娜现在学习什么？她的专业是什么？
3. 安娜为什么学习中文？
4. 林舟和安娜的学习兴趣有什么不同？
5. 你跟谁更像？为什么？

### 4. See the words again

1. 我现在住在西安，目前在大学学习。
2. 她的工作比较忙，但是她很喜欢。
3. 我的专业是历史，我对中国历史很有兴趣。
4. 看电影是我的爱好，学语言也是我的爱好。
5. 比起写汉字，我更喜欢练习说中文。

### 5. Controlled practice

Choose the more natural target: `现在 / 目前`, `兴趣 / 爱好`, `比较 / 更`.
1. 我________在北京工作，明年可能去上海。
2. A：你对什么有________？ B：我对音乐很感兴趣。
3. 跑步是我的一个________。
4. 这本书________容易，那本书很难。
5. 我喜欢茶，但是我________喜欢咖啡。

Combine the ideas.
6. 我在工作。我的工作很忙。我喜欢这份工作。→ Use `现在`, `比较`, and `但是`.
7. 我学习中文。我想练习口语。→ Use `更`.

### 6. English → Mandarin

1. I currently work at a company.
2. What is your major?
3. I am interested in languages, and studying Chinese is also my hobby.
4. My work is relatively busy, but I like it.
5. Compared with writing, I prefer speaking Chinese.

### 7. Create and speak

1. Describe your current work or studies in three sentences.
2. Make two different sentences about one `兴趣` and one `爱好`.
3. Compare two activities with `比较` and `更`.
4. Roleplay: ask a new classmate about work, studies, major, and interests. Find one similarity and one difference.

**Exit ticket:** Say what you currently do, what interests you, and what you prefer.

## Day 3 🟡 — 平时和周末

- **New targets:** 觉得 / 因为 / 所以 / 但是 / 还是 / 一般 / 经常 / 平时 / 周末 / 计划
- **Recycle:** 工作 / 学习 / 兴趣 / 喜欢 / 比较 / 更
- **Patterns:** `因为…所以…` · `A还是B？` · expressing a contrast with `但是`
- **Required output:** 30-second routine; weekend negotiation
- **Repetition goal:** connect ideas with `因为…所以…` and `但是`; ask a choice with `还是`
- **Finish when:** you can describe a usual routine and a future plan with a reason

### 1. Retrieval warm-up

1. Say what you currently do and what your major or specialty is.
2. Name two interests and say which one you like more.
3. Ask someone what they like about their work or studies.

### 2. Read first: characters only

- 林舟：你平时工作和学习都很忙吗？
- 安娜：工作日比较忙，因为白天要上课，所以我一般晚上复习。
- 林舟：你经常在家学习，还是去图书馆？
- 安娜：我平时在家学，但是周末经常去图书馆。你周末一般做什么？
- 林舟：我喜欢运动，但是最近工作忙，运动比较少。
- 安娜：那你这个周末有什么计划？
- 林舟：我觉得天气好的话，可以先去跑步，再找朋友吃饭。
- 安娜：这个计划不错，因为运动和见朋友都很重要。

**Mini glossary:** 工作日 weekday · 白天 daytime · 复习 review · 图书馆 library · 运动 exercise

### 3. Dialogue questions

1. 安娜为什么一般晚上复习？
2. 她平时在哪儿学习？周末呢？
3. 林舟最近为什么运动比较少？
4. 林舟这个周末有什么计划？
5. 你觉得谁的周末更像你的周末？请说明原因。

### 4. See the words again

1. 我觉得早上学习比较好，因为那时候很安静。
2. 因为我周末有时间，所以我经常跟朋友见面。
3. 我喜欢出去，但是今天还是想在家休息。
4. 你平时坐公交车还是开车？
5. 我一般周五做周末计划，周六再决定去哪儿。

### 5. Controlled practice

Fill in the blanks. Use each connector once: `因为 / 所以 / 但是 / 还是`.
1. 我喜欢这份工作，________有时候比较忙。
2. ________今天下雨，________我们打算在家看电影。
3. 你周末想休息，________想跟朋友出去？

Transform or combine.
4. 我工作很忙。我每天学习中文。→ Use `但是`.
5. 周末我有时间。我经常做饭。→ Use `因为…所以…`.
6. Write a question contrasting two plans with `还是`.

### 6. English → Mandarin

1. I usually study at night.
2. Do you often work on weekends?
3. Because I am busy today, I plan to call my friend tomorrow.
4. I like going out, but this weekend I still want to rest at home.
5. Do you think we should drink coffee or eat together?

### 7. Create and speak

1. Describe a normal weekday using `平时`, `一般`, and `经常`.
2. Describe this weekend’s plan and give one reason.
3. Create one `还是` question, then answer it yourself in two sentences.
4. Roleplay: you and a friend have different weekend preferences. Discuss them and agree on one plan.

**Exit ticket:** Speak for 30 seconds about your usual routine and next weekend.

## Day 4 🟡 — 以后保持联系

- **New targets:** 打算 / 以后 / 已经 / 还 / 如果 / 方便 / 一起 / 联系 / 朋友 / 同事
- **Recycle:** 认识 / 介绍 / 兴趣 / 周末 / 计划
- **Patterns:** `已经…了，还…` · `如果…的话，就…` · `以后可以一起…`
- **Required output:** introduce another person; invite them and handle a scheduling conflict
- **Repetition goal:** contrast `已经` and `还`; use `如果…方便的话…`
- **Finish when:** you can exchange contact information and arrange another time naturally

### 1. Retrieval warm-up

1. Describe one weekday habit and one weekend habit.
2. Explain a weekend plan with `因为…所以…`.
3. Ask a choice question with `还是`.

### 2. Read first: characters only

- 林舟：安娜，这是我的同事小顾。小顾，这是我的朋友安娜。
- 小顾：你好，很高兴认识你。林舟已经介绍过你了。
- 安娜：你好！听说你也对语言有兴趣，还经常练习英语。
- 小顾：对，我打算以后学习西班牙语。你还在学中文吗？
- 安娜：还在学。如果你方便的话，我们周末可以一起练习。
- 小顾：好啊。我周六已经有计划了，但是周日下午方便。
- 林舟：那你们先加联系方式，以后就可以直接联系了。
- 安娜：好。认识新朋友，还能一起学习，真不错。

**Mini glossary:** 介绍过 have introduced · 西班牙语 Spanish · 直接 directly · 真不错 really nice

### 3. Dialogue questions

1. 小顾是林舟的朋友还是同事？
2. 小顾打算以后学习什么？
3. 安娜建议她们周末一起做什么？
4. 小顾什么时候已经有计划？什么时候方便？
5. 如果你是小顾，你会怎么跟安娜保持联系？

### 4. See the words again

1. 我已经认识他了，但是还不认识他的同事。
2. 我打算周末联系朋友，一起吃饭。
3. 如果你明天方便的话，我们就一起喝咖啡。
4. 她是我的朋友，也是我以前的同事。
5. 我们已经交换了联系方式，以后可以常联系。

### 5. Controlled practice

Fill in the blanks.
1. 我________做完今天的工作了，但是________要复习中文。
2. 你周末________做什么？
3. ________你方便的话，我们________去吃饭吧。
4. 他不是我的________，是跟我一起工作的一位________。

Combine the ideas.
5. 周六不方便。周日见面。→ Use `但是` and `方便`.
6. We have each other’s contact information. We can talk later. → Express with `已经` and `以后` in Mandarin.

### 6. English → Mandarin

1. I already know her, but I do not know her colleague yet.
2. I plan to contact a friend this weekend.
3. If you are free, we can study together.
4. I already have plans on Saturday. Is Sunday convenient?
5. Let’s exchange contact information and keep in touch later.

### 7. Create and speak

1. Say three things you have already done and two things you still plan to do.
2. Invite someone using `如果你方便的话…`.
3. Introduce one friend or colleague in four sentences.
4. Roleplay: two new acquaintances discover a shared interest, exchange contact information, and arrange a time to meet.

**Exit ticket:** Invite a friend to do something, respond with a scheduling problem, and suggest another time.

## Day 5 🔴 — 介绍一个人

- **Focus:** cumulative reuse of all 40 targets
- **Goal:** describe and compare people without following a memorized script
- **Required output:** six-sentence third-person introduction; four follow-up questions
- **Repetition goal:** combine identity, work or study, interests, routine, and plans
- **Finish when:** you can introduce someone for 45 seconds without a script

### 1. Rapid retrieval

Say one complete sentence for each cue:
1. `来自 + 住在`
2. `目前 + 工作`
3. `专业 + 兴趣`
4. `平时 + 经常`
5. `周末 + 打算`
6. `如果 + 方便`

### 2. Read first: characters only

- 安娜：小顾，我想把我的朋友美玲介绍给你。她来自新加坡，现在住在上海。
- 小顾：她目前在工作，还是在学习？
- 安娜：她已经开始工作了，在一家银行工作，不过周末还学习中文。
- 小顾：她为什么想学中文？是因为工作吗？
- 安娜：工作是一个原因，但是她自己也对语言有兴趣。
- 小顾：真巧！我平时也喜欢学语言。她还有什么爱好？
- 安娜：她经常跑步，也喜欢做饭，不过比起运动，她更喜欢跟朋友一起吃饭。
- 小顾：如果她这个周末方便，我们可以联系她，一起喝咖啡。

**Mini glossary:** 新加坡 Singapore · 银行 bank · 原因 reason · 真巧 what a coincidence

### 3. Dialogue questions

1. 美玲来自哪儿？现在住在哪儿？
2. 她目前做什么？周末还做什么？
3. 她为什么学习中文？
4. 比起运动，她更喜欢什么？
5. 小顾最后提出了什么计划？

### 4. Accuracy practice

Correct each sentence aloud.
1. 我来自从美国。
2. 我目前是工作在上海。
3. 因为我喜欢中文，所以但是我每天学习。
4. 我已经还认识他的朋友了。
5. 如果你方便，所以我们一起吃饭。

### 5. English → Mandarin

1. This is my colleague. Let me introduce her.
2. She is from Singapore and currently lives in Shanghai.
3. She works at a bank, but she still studies Chinese on weekends.
4. Because she is interested in languages, she often practices with friends.
5. If she is free this weekend, we plan to invite her for coffee.

### 6. Create and speak

1. Introduce a real or invented person in six connected sentences.
2. Include origin, current location, work or study, interests, routine, and a future plan.
3. Compare that person with yourself using `比较` or `更`.
4. Ask four follow-up questions about another person’s introduction.
5. Roleplay: introduce two people. They must find two shared interests and make one plan.

**Exit ticket:** Give a 45-second introduction of someone without notes.

## Day 6 🔴 — 从认识到约下次见面

- **Focus:** cumulative performance and flexible conversation
- **Goal:** sustain a four-to-six-minute conversation using Unit 1 vocabulary
- **Required output:** two-minute introduction; six questions; roleplay with a scheduling twist
- **Repetition goal:** reuse at least 12 targets, including words from all four focus groups
- **Finish when:** you can sustain the conversation and recover when the first plan fails

### 1. Retrieval challenge

Translate aloud without writing:
1. My name is…, and I am from…
2. I currently work/study…
3. I am interested in…, but I prefer…
4. I usually…, so this weekend I plan to…
5. If you are free, we can…together.

### 2. Read first: characters only

- 陈明：你好，这儿有人坐吗？我叫陈明，第一次来参加这个活动。
- 安娜：你好，我叫安娜。这里没人坐。你来自哪儿？
- 陈明：我来自成都，不过现在住在苏州，目前在上海工作。你呢？
- 安娜：我从加拿大来，现在在上海学习中文。你平时也学语言吗？
- 陈明：学一点儿。因为工作比较忙，所以我一般周末学习。你有什么爱好？
- 安娜：我喜欢看电影和跑步，但是最近更喜欢跟朋友一起做饭。
- 陈明：我也喜欢做饭！如果你周日方便，我们可以一起去参加一个活动。
- 安娜：周日我已经有计划了，不过周六还方便。我们先加联系方式吧。

**Mini glossary:** 有人坐 occupied · 第一次 the first time · 参加 participate · 活动 event

### 3. Dialogue questions

1. 陈明是不是第一次来这里？他来做什么？
2. 他来自哪儿、住在哪儿、在哪儿工作？
3. 安娜现在做什么？
4. 两个人有什么共同爱好？
5. 他们为什么不能周日一起参加活动？最后可能怎么安排？

### 4. English → Mandarin

1. Nice to meet you. Is this your first time here?
2. I am from Chengdu, but I currently live in Suzhou and work in Shanghai.
3. Because work is relatively busy, I usually study on weekends.
4. I like movies, but recently I prefer cooking with friends.
5. I already have plans on Sunday. If Saturday is convenient, contact me.

### 5. Independent production

Complete all three rounds. Do not reuse exactly the same sentences.

#### Round 1 — Two-minute introduction
Include: name, origin, current location, work/study, interests, routine, and one plan.

#### Round 2 — Follow-up questions
Ask at least six questions. Include a name question, an origin question, a choice question with `还是`, and a question about future plans.

#### Round 3 — Roleplay with a twist
Meet someone new and discover a shared interest. Invite them somewhere. Your first suggested time is not convenient, so negotiate another time and exchange contact information.

### 6. Self-check

Can you:
- Use at least 12 Unit 1 targets without looking?
- Answer in three or four connected sentences?
- Give a reason with `因为…所以…`?
- Compare preferences with `比较` or `更`?
- Recover when a proposed time is not convenient?
- Ask a natural question back?

Write down three words that still feel slow. Use each in a new sentence, then say those sentences twice.

### After the unit

Record:

- Five words you can use quickly
- Three words that still feel slow
- Two grammar patterns to recycle next week
- One recurring speaking error to retest
